### Line 80: avoid double "presuppose"

**Before**: ...The Coffin Texts attestations preserve the same oblique-allusive register as the Pyramid Texts but presuppose a more elaborated narrative tradition: ...and the spells presuppose familiarity with the eye-injury, the testicle-injury, the divine tribunal, and the eventual award of the kingship to Horus.

**After**: ...The Coffin Texts attestations preserve the same oblique-allusive register as the Pyramid Texts but presuppose a more elaborated narrative tradition: ...and the spells take for granted familiarity with the eye-injury, the testicle-injury, the divine tribunal, and the eventual award of the kingship to Horus.

**Reason**: Two "presuppose"s in one sentence; second instance becomes "take for granted" to vary diction.

### Line 131: passive nominalization

**Before**: ...if Seth were a "chaos-figure" in the simple sense of Western-philosophical inchoate primordial unformedness, his annihilation would be theologically required for the establishment of cosmic order...

**After**: ...if Seth were a "chaos-figure" in the simple sense of Western-philosophical inchoate primordial unformedness, theology would require his annihilation to establish cosmic order...

**Reason**: Recasts a double passive ("his annihilation would be theologically required for the establishment") into an active "theology would require... to establish"; halves the syllable count without changing meaning.
