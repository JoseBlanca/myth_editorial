### Line 4: zombie phrase

**Before**: He hooks it on a baited line and is on the verge of striking the killing-blow when the giant Hymir cuts the line.

**After**: He hooks it on a baited line and is about to strike the killing-blow when the giant Hymir cuts the line.

**Reason**: "is on the verge of striking" → "is about to strike"; one fewer nominalisation, identical meaning, more direct in a narrative-summary opener.

### Line 123: zombie phrase

**Before**: The encircling serpent's cosmic-boundary position has the consequence that any combat between Thor and Jörmungandr is structurally a combat at and for the boundary of the human world. The serpent's defeat unmakes the boundary, and the boundary's unmaking is the structural feature that makes the Ragnarök combat the eschatological close of the Norse cosmic age.

**After**: The encircling serpent's cosmic-boundary position makes any combat between Thor and Jörmungandr structurally a combat at and for the boundary of the human world. The serpent's defeat unmakes the boundary, and that unmaking is what turns the Ragnarök combat into the eschatological close of the Norse cosmic age.

**Reason**: "has the consequence that" and "is the structural feature that makes" are both zombie-noun stacks. Replace with "makes... structurally a combat..." and "is what turns... into..."; same logical structure, far less load on the reader.

### Line 276: paragraph rhythm

**Before**: ...At least three named skalds across two centuries composed verse on the encounter, and the encounter therefore has the documentary profile...

**After**: ...At least three named skalds across two centuries composed verse on the encounter, which therefore has the documentary profile...

**Reason**: "and the encounter therefore" repeats "the encounter"; relative pronoun ties the second clause more tightly.
